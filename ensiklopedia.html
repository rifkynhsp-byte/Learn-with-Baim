How it works

Baim hears an English word and taps the matching picture. When he gets it right, the card shows the word and its Bahasa Indonesia meaning, and says both out loud.

Levels 1 to 5 are vocabulary from the Cambridge Pre A1 Starters and A1 Movers wordlists. Level 6 is listening comprehension: a spoken question with three answers, like the Starters listening paper.

No guessing

Two wrong taps and the game asks him to find the right one twice in a row before moving on. Choices are reshuffled after every single tap, so position never helps.

Voices

This game uses the voices already on the device: English for the word, Bahasa Indonesia for the meaning. Nothing is downloaded.
