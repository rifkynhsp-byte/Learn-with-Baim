# Rumah Belajar

A learning app for one five year old, built by his parents.

Baim is preparing for primary school in Indonesia after starting out in
Australia. This app covers reading, English, science, counting and handwriting
in one place, using the Cambridge, Australian NSW and Indonesian Kurikulum
Merdeka stages as its yardstick. It is free for any family to copy and use, and
the names are changeable in Settings.

    Created with love and care by Papi Rifky and Mommy Keke
    for beloved Abang Baim and Ade Yahya.

## Membaca — reading Bahasa Indonesia

Uses **Metode Suku Kata**, the syllable method used in Indonesian schools. The
game says a word, then its syllables, and Baim drags the right syllable bubbles
into place in order.

- **634 words across 6 levels**: two open syllables, then closed endings, then
  the `ng` and `ny` sounds, then three syllables, long words, and dinosaurs.
- **16 habitats** drive the backdrop and the science: laut, sungai, danau, rawa,
  gua, hutan, savana, gurun, kutub, gunung, langit, sawah, kota, rumah,
  keluarga, zaman purba. Finish a word and it tells you where that animal lives.
- **1,750 recorded Indonesian audio clips** in the `audio/` folder, made with a
  human diphone voice. Every syllable, every word, and a blend clip that reads
  "ku, da, … kuda" in one breath.
- **Only correct answers are ever spoken.** Distractors are never voiced, so he
  cannot learn a wrong sound.
- The picture starts as a black silhouette and opens up as each syllable lands.
- Family words show your own photos.

## English — Cambridge Starters and Movers

Reversed from Membaca: he **hears an English word and taps the picture**.

| Level | Content | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Animals and family | 3 |
| 2 | Food, body, home, school, doing words | 5 |
| 3 | Places, the world, jobs, feelings, opposites | 6 |
| 4 | Listening: what, where, who, how many | 3 |
| 5 | Talking: what do you say? | 3 |
| 6 | Thinking: riddles and clues | 3 |

Vocabulary comes from the official Cambridge Pre A1 Starters and A1 Movers
wordlists. From level 2 the wrong pictures are drawn from the **same topic**, so
a lion sits beside a tiger and a zebra and shape matching stops working.

Every correct answer shows and speaks the **Bahasa Indonesia meaning**.

## Ensiklopedia — the animal encyclopedia

Built on a database of **140 species**, each stored once with verified fields:
Latin binomial, class, legs, body covering, diet, habitat, baby name and
conservation status. Every question is generated from that one record, so no two
facts can disagree.

| Level | | Items |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the animals | 197 |
| 2 | Latin names, spoken slowly | 140 |
| 3 | Bodies: legs, skin, babies | 418 |
| 4 | Science: group, diet, habitat, eggs or milk | 560 |
| 5 | Nature: endangered status and how we care | 192 |
| 6 | World: space, body, floating, magnets, inventions, history | 147 |
| 7 | Story time | 42 |
| 8 | Grand challenge: reverse Latin plus mixed review | 320 |
| 9 | Indonesia: islands, food, batik, wayang, Borobudur, Pancasila | 27 |
| 10 | Our world and how we treat it | 23 |

**Latin** is spoken genus, pause, species, pause, then the whole name, so he
hears *Panthera* and *tigris* as two ideas. Indonesian species are deliberately
included: anoa, babirusa, komodo, pesut, badak jawa, bekantan, owa jawa,
tarsius, trenggiling.

**Story time** has eight public domain tales heard scene by scene, each ending
with a comprehension question and a question about the lesson.

## Berhitung — counting through food webs

Subtraction is not an abstract operation here. **Adding brings more of the same
group. Taking away is a predator eating.** Five rabbits, the fox eats two: the
two eaten rabbits grey out and fall away, and two foxes are drawn, so the
picture and the sum agree.

Twelve levels: count, add to 5, eaten, both to 10, missing number (`? + 4 = 9`),
two steps, sharing, food web sense, bigger numbers, doubles and halves, which
sign, story problems.

The food web is real: grass to rabbit to fox, seaweed to small fish to shark.
After every answer it shows the chain and a fact about producers, herbivores and
carnivores. "The fox eats all six rabbits" has the answer 0, and so does "all the
grass is gone, how many rabbits stay?"

## Menulis — handwriting

Ruled lines like a handwriting book, a dashed model letter, and real pressure
sensitivity from the Apple Pencil.

Sixteen levels, grouped by the hand movement they share rather than
alphabetically: strokes and shapes, round letters (c o a d g q), tall letters,
the rest, capitals, numbers, **drawing emoji**, suku kata, Indonesian words,
English words, longer words, family names, Indonesian sentences, English
sentences, numbers as words, and more drawing.

- **Each letter is measured separately.** Writing "sap" does not complete
  "sapi"; all four letters must be traced.
- **Nothing is ever locked.** Next is always available, and he is paid for
  whatever he actually traced.
- Stars come from a match score combining how much of each letter he covered
  with how much ink landed off it: 92% and up four stars, 80% three, 66% two,
  below that one.

## Koleksi — zoo, pets and the parent view

**The zoo.** 50 animals as dark silhouettes with a locked XP cost. Each costs
about 18% more than the last: ant 40, cat 75, frog 180, shrimp 910, monkey
2,050, whale 10,500, orangutan 55,500, kangaroo 107,000, koala 126,000. Early
animals arrive in the first days. The koala is a year's trophy.

**The pets.** Four pets that grow through stages: kitten to young cat to grown
cat to big wild cat; egg to chick to hen to rooster; frogspawn to tadpole to
frog; seed to sprout to tree. Feeding costs food, and food is only earned by
answering. No learning, no growth.

**The parent tab.** Total XP, questions answered, animals collected, days
practised, XP per day for the last week, and a grade band.

## How points work

Points are paid for **quality, not volume**.

    first time right      full marks
    second try            under half
    third try or more     almost nothing

Clean first-time answers build a streak multiplier, capped at 1.6x. Any wrong
answer resets it, so it cannot be farmed by guessing.

Difficulty is weighted, so grinding easy levels does not climb the ladder:

    level 1 -> 3 XP     level 5 -> 15 XP     level 10 -> 39 XP
    level 2 -> 5 XP     level 6 -> 19 XP     level 12 -> 52 XP
    level 4 -> 12 XP    level 8 -> 29 XP

### Grade bands

At a solid 40 answers a day:

| Band | Reached after |
|---|---|
| Kindy A | start |
| Kindy B | about 12 days of level 2 work |
| Preparatory | about 25 days of level 3 work |
| Primary Grade 1 | about 33 days of level 4 work |
| Primary Grade 2 | about 48 days of level 5 work |
| Primary Grade 3 | about 62 days of level 6 work |
| Grades 4, 5, 6 | 70,000 then 100,000 then 140,000 XP |

These are a rough reference, not a test result. They say which kind of work he is
comfortably handling, using the Cambridge, NSW and Kurikulum Merdeka stages as
the yardstick.

## No guessing

Across every game:

- Two wrong taps and he must find the right answer **twice in a row**.
- Choices reshuffle after **every single tap**, so position never helps.
- A wrong option never shares the correct answer's picture.
- Distractors come from the same topic or the same animal group.

## Settings

The gear key on the bottom strip, on every page. Change the child, the father,
the mother and the sibling, and the whole app follows: every prompt, every line
of praise, every device voice. Made so another family can use it.

The recorded Indonesian audio still says the original names, because those are
files rather than speech.

## The strip

A Touch Bar style row along the bottom of every game: contextual keys on the
left, then Home, Settings, the live streak, total XP, and your pet with its food
count. Tap the pet to jump to the zoo.

## Sound

Membaca plays the recorded clips in `audio/`. Everything else uses the voices
already on the device: English for English, Bahasa Indonesia for meanings. On
iPad, add the Indonesian voice under Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content,
Voices. Meanings always appear on screen either way.

## Publishing

Upload every file in this folder to a GitHub repository, then
**Settings, Pages, main / root**. Keep the `audio/` folder alongside.

Works offline once loaded, apart from the web font. Best on an iPad held in
landscape.

## Editing the content

Each game is a single HTML file with its data at the top of the script. Search
for `const WORDS`, `const QUIZ` or `const LEVELS` and add rows in the same shape.
New Indonesian words fall back to the device voice, since their audio clips do
not exist yet.
